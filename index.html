Our Expertise

Comprehensive Practice Areas in Gqeberha

We offer expert legal representation across a broad spectrum of South African law.

Medical Negligence

Claims for surgical errors, hospital misdiagnosis, and birth injury matters to secure rightful compensation against medical practitioners.

RAF Matters

Road Accident Fund compensation claims for motor vehicle accident victims, passenger injuries, driver claims, and loss of support.

Divorce Matters

Contested and uncontested divorce proceedings, marital property division, accrual claims, and spousal maintenance.

Maintenance & Custody

Child custody arrangements, parental rights and responsibilities, care & contact disputes, and maintenance claims.

Criminal Litigation

Bail applications, trial defense, criminal appeals, and representation in Regional and High Courts across Gqeberha.

Civil Litigation

Magistrate & High Court litigation, contractual disputes, damages claims, evictions, and interdicts.

Administration of Estates

Deceased estates winding-up, drafting wills, Master of the High Court reporting, and trust setup.

Unlawful Arrest

Claims against the Minister of Police and law enforcement for unlawful arrest, detention, and police brutality.

Debt Collection

Commercial and individual debt recovery services, letters of demand, summons execution, and financial recovery.
